# 🌕 SELENOVIX
### Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer

**Train for the Moon. Lead the Mission.**

SELENOVIX is an interactive lunar mission training simulator designed to help young learners understand the challenges of operating a human outpost near the Moon's South Pole.

Through simulated mission scenarios, astronauts-in-training learn to manage essential resources, monitor crew safety, respond to environmental hazards, and make critical decisions that affect mission survival.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Establishing a sustainable human presence on the Moon requires careful planning, resource management, environmental awareness, and effective decision-making.

SELENOVIX transforms these challenges into an interactive training experience.

Users take the role of a lunar mission commander and learn how decisions involving oxygen, water, food, power, solar radiation, and lunar dust can affect crew safety and outpost survival.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Introduce students to the challenges of lunar exploration.
- Demonstrate the importance of managing limited mission resources.
- Explain environmental hazards around the lunar South Pole.
- Encourage scientific thinking and responsible decision-making.
- Make space exploration education interactive and accessible.

## 🛰️ Key Features

### 1. Mission Dashboard
Monitor the simulated lunar outpost, including:
- Oxygen reserves
- Water supplies
- Food supplies
- Power reserves
- Mission timer
- Environmental hazard indicators

### 2. Crew Health Monitor
View simulated astronaut health indicators, including:
- Heart rate
- Oxygen level
- Body temperature

### 3. Solar Radiation Monitoring
Explore solar radiation hazards through a training simulation supported by real NASA space-weather event information.

### 4. Lunar Dust Hazard Simulation
Understand how lunar dust can affect astronaut suits, equipment, and solar panels.

### 5. Emergency Center
Practice responding to simulated mission emergencies and review suggested actions.

### 6. Mission Decision Center
Make decisions in four interactive emergency scenarios:

- ☀️ Solar Radiation Emergency
- 🫁 Oxygen Supply Emergency
- 🌑 Lunar Dust Emergency
- ⚡ Outpost Power Failure

### 7. Mission Consequence Engine
The engine evaluates decisions and calculates their simulated effects on mission performance and crew safety.

### 8. Mission Performance Report
Receive a final performance score after completing all four training scenarios.

## 🌍 NASA Data Integration

SELENOVIX uses NASA-related scientific resources to provide real space-science context.

### NASA CCMC DONKI

The Space Weather Database Of Notifications, Knowledge, Information (DONKI) provides information about recorded space-weather events, including:

- Solar flares
- Coronal mass ejections (CMEs)

SELENOVIX retrieves recent event information and displays it in the Mission Dashboard and Mission Decision Center.

Source: https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/tools/DONKI/

### NASA Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter – CRaTER

The Cosmic Ray Telescope for the Effects of Radiation (CRaTER) investigates the lunar radiation environment and the effects of radiation behind tissue-equivalent material.

SELENOVIX references the CRaTER scientific dataset to provide context for lunar radiation research.

Source: https://pds.nasa.gov/ds-view/pds/viewDataset.jsp?dsid=LRO-L-CRAT-3-CDR-CALIBRATED-V1.0

**Scientific accuracy note:** DONKI event counts do not represent astronaut radiation exposure or radiation dose at the lunar South Pole. CRaTER's orbital observations are not real-time measurements of astronaut dose at a future outpost. SELENOVIX uses simulated radiation hazard values for training.

## 🧪 Simulation and Scientific Limitations

SELENOVIX is an educational training simulator, not an operational spacecraft control system.

- Mission resource levels are simulated.
- Crew health indicators are simulated.
- Emergency scenarios and their consequences are simulated.
- Radiation hazard values are simulated training indicators.
- NASA DONKI observations provide real space-weather event context.
- CRaTER provides scientific lunar radiation research context.

The application does not calculate actual astronaut radiation dose, predict real lunar weather, or control spacecraft systems.
